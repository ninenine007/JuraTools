Act for the Establishment of and Procedure for Labour Court B.E. 2522, s.49.

No statutory formula. Under s.49 the Labour Court has discretion to order reinstatement or — if the parties can no longer work together — damages, weighing the employee's age, length of service, hardship on termination, the cause of termination, and compensation otherwise due.
