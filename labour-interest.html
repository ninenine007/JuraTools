Default interest runs from the due date until paid. Use 15%/yr for the LPA s.9 ¶1 items (severance, pay-in-lieu, holiday pay) or 5%/yr for ordinary debts (CCC s.224). เงินเพิ่ม (s.9 ¶2) adds 15% of the principal per completed 7-day period, but only on willful non-payment without reasonable cause.
