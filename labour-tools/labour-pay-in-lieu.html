Payment in Lieu of Advance Notice

สินจ้างแทนการบอกกล่าวล่วงหน้า
Under s.17, notice on an indefinite contract is tied to the wage-payment cycle: notice given on or before a pay day takes effect on the next pay day, capped at 3 months. End the job without that notice and the employer owes pay-in-lieu for the missing period (s.17/1).

Pay-in-lieu, if notice is skipped

Wages from the termination effective date through the step 2 date (when lawful notice would end), both days counted (s.17/1).
A day worked or paid is owed as wage, never pay-in-lieu: termination (s.118 ¶2) happens only when the employer stops both the work and the wages, so if the dismissal day was worked or paid the termination effective date is the next day even when the letter names that day. Pay-in-lieu day 1 = the termination effective date. Garden leave with wages running to the notice date needs no pay-in-lieu at all.
