Court form (๙)

Attorney Appointment — form (๙)

ใบแต่งทนายความ — แบบพิมพ์ศาล (๙)

Fill the firm's own form and export Word .docx. Two precedents used exactly as they are — JDA for a defendant (“การถอนคำให้การ”) and PY for a plaintiff (“การถอนฟ้อง”); the s.62 wording and the office address on the back page are never touched. Each value is typed into the original Word run, so font, underline and language stay as they were, and padded to sit where the firm's file had it. Widths are measured against TH SarabunPSK, so a line that would wrap — and push the form past two pages — is flagged before export; condensed spacing is used only as a last resort, on that one value. One form per client and per lawyer, a live page preview, and a lawyer register kept on your own machine.

Supporting document

Certified ID Copy

สำเนาบัตรประจำตัวประชาชน — รับรองสำเนาถูกต้อง

Drop in a scan or phone photo of the real card, drag four corners and it is straightened to its true 85.6 × 54 mm (ISO/IEC 7810 ID-1) — no stretched or squashed cards. The “ใช้สำหรับ … เท่านั้น” band is set at the original’s −28° over each card; the certification block, optional date, and black-out bars for religion or the laser code follow. Saves a one-page A4 Word .docx in TH SarabunPSK, every card, band and signature block anchored to the page in millimetres so Word cannot shift it, with a warning when the source scan is too low-resolution. Images and names are never stored.

Litigation document

Power of Attorney to Sue

หนังสือมอบอำนาจให้ฟ้องคดี

The firm's own template, filled and exported as Word .docx. The fixed wording — the grant “ร่วมกัน และ/หรือ แยกกัน”, the eight powers and the ratification — is never touched; the grantor (a company or individuals), the attorneys, the counterparty, the courts and the cause are typed into the template's original runs. Add clauses anywhere in the list. The signature table is built from the firm's own rows for any number of signers — pairs, with the odd one out across the full width, names centred on their lines. Stamp duty is worked out under schedule item 7(ค) — 30 baht per attorney — and handed to the stamp-duty calculator. A live preview, fictional examples, and a brief to have an AI draft the matter; also available to AI as the MCP tool create_power_of_attorney.
