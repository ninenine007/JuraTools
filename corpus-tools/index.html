Contract Concordance

คลังข้อความสัญญา — สืบค้นถ้อยคำในบริบท

Search a corpus built from your own contracts. KWIC concordance centres every hit in its context and sorts by the words around it; the clause library ranks every termination or indemnity clause by how often that exact formulation recurs, so the house standard rises to the top; collocations show what habitually surrounds a term. Thai word segmentation is precomputed by PyThaiNLP, so Thai and English are searched with equal precision.

Contract Corpus Builder

เครื่องมือสร้างคลังข้อความจากสัญญา

A Python pipeline that turns a folder of .docx contracts and Azure Document Intelligence output into the corpus file the Concordance reads. Normalises Thai text, joins OCR line wraps, segments clauses and zones, tokenises with PyThaiNLP over a legal dictionary, and reports per-document quality. It runs on your machine and calls nothing.

Source & README

How the two fit together

  1. Put your contracts in a folder. For scanned PDFs, run them through Azure Document Intelligence yourself and save the JSON beside them — the pipeline reads that output and never calls any service.
  2. Run the builder, then read the QC report it prints — documents it could not place are flagged, never dropped. python build.py --stage all --in data/raw --out data/corpus.jtcorpus.gz
  3. Open the Concordance and load that corpus file. It is parsed once and kept in your browser.
  4. Search, sort by neighbouring words, and read the clause library. Export a selection when you want it, with optional pseudonymisation of names and ID numbers.

Your contracts stay on your machine. The builder runs locally and makes no network request. The Concordance holds the corpus in your browser only — there is no server, no upload, and no analytics. Corpus files are client material and are excluded from this repository.
