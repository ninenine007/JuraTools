Registers · CCC §§1222 · 1224

CorpDesk — Share Register Workbench

สมุดทะเบียนผู้ถือหุ้นแบบ replay

Full share-register lifecycle in one file. Set up the initial cap table, then drop transaction cards on a pile — transfers, capital increases (§1222 pro-rata rights), capital decreases (§1224), paid-up updates, and renumbering. The replay engine derives the current shareholding, certificate history, and per-shareholder ledgers. Exports master-data JSON for Thai share documents.
