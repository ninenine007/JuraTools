↔ บีบตัวอักษรที่อยู่ · Condense the address

The address in the body of the certificate has one line to itself and does not wrap. A long one is squeezed into it instead of overrunning — Word's “Condensed by”, in points. The two lines are set apart because they run out of room apart. Leave this off unless an address actually overruns.

🕰 As at this certificate's issue date

A register spans years. Turn this on for a certificate issued before a capital increase or a change of board, and it keeps the figures and the signatures it was actually issued with.

Export & data

ออกเอกสารและข้อมูล
📄 Word document

One file per company, one page per certificate — the same shape as the sets the firm already keeps. Several companies open at once export as a .zip, one Word file each.

📗 Batch from Excel

Download the sheet, fill one row per certificate, upload it back. Uploading replaces the certificate list; the company and directors stay as they are.

🔗 ส่งต่อข้อมูล · Company & people

The open company and its whole person directory, passed to the other corporate tools through one shared slot in this browser — and taken back the same way. People already known by the same name are not duplicated. Nothing leaves this device.

💾 Backup

Every company, its certificates and its person directory live in this browser only. One backup file holds all of them — restore puts them all back.

⚠️ Danger zone

Deletes every certificate, the company details and every saved person on this device. Cannot be undone.

Cancels this certificate and issues new ones in its place — one to the transferee, and one back to the transferor for any balance. They are added to the end of the register, which is how the firm's own sets are ordered.
