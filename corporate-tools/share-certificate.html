Export & data

📄 Word document

One file per company, one page per certificate — the same shape as the sets the firm already keeps. Several companies open at once export as a .zip, one Word file each.

📗 Batch from Excel

Download the sheet, fill one row per certificate, upload it back. Uploading replaces the certificate list; the company and directors stay as they are.

💾 Backup

Every company, its certificates and its person directory live in this browser only. One backup file holds all of them — restore puts them all back.

⚠️ Danger zone

Deletes every certificate, the company details and every saved person on this device. Cannot be undone.
