Default Interest Calculator

ดอกเบี้ยผิดนัดตาม ป.พ.พ. มาตรา 224/224-1 — auto-splits the statutory rate change of 11 April 2021 (7.5% → 5% p.a.), supports contractual rates, yearly / monthly / daily rates with calendar or day-count duration bases, direct duration input, and multiple debts or defaulted installments with a per-segment breakdown.

1 · Debts in default
Duration converts as: 1 year = 12 months = 365 days and 1 month = 30 days (used only where the rate period and your inputs differ, e.g. days remainder under a yearly rate).
s.224/1 (installments): when a debt is payable in installments and one installment is in default, default interest may only be charged on the defaulted installment's principal — not the whole outstanding balance. Add each defaulted installment as its own line with its own default date.
2 · Interest rate timeline
Statutory (auto)
Duration mode has no dates, so the statutory 2021 rate split cannot apply — one rate is used for the whole duration. Switch to date mode for the 7.5% → 5% auto-split.
Statutory default rate: before 11 Apr 2021 the default rate was 7.5% p.a.; from 11 Apr 2021 it is the s.7 rate (3%) + 2% = 5% p.a., applied to interest accruing from that date even for earlier defaults. The s.7 base rate is adjustable by royal decree — add a rate change here if it moves. If the creditor may lawfully claim a higher rate (e.g. a contractual rate), that rate continues during default (s.224 ¶1): use a flat preset or edit the rows. Rates here are read as % per the rate period chosen in section 3.
3 · Calculation
Each interest line is rounded with the chosen method and precision; subtotals and totals are sums of the rounded lines, so the statement always adds up.
