Corpus Tools

Read your own executed contracts as a corpus — the Contract Concordance (คลังข้อความสัญญา) centres every hit in its context and sorts by the words around it, ranks every clause of a kind by how often that exact formulation recurs so the house standard rises to the top, and reports what habitually surrounds a term. Fed by a local Corpus Builder that reads .docx and Azure Document Intelligence output, reconstructs Word clause numbering, and segments Thai with PyThaiNLP. Your contracts never leave the machine.
