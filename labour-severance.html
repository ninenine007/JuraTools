Severance Pay Calculator

ค่าชดเชย

Labour Protection Act B.E. 2541, s.118 (tiers, post-2019 Amendment No.7), excluded by s.119.

1

The employment

Who worked, until when, and for what wage.
Termination (s.118 ¶2) happens only when the employer stops both the work and the wages. Garden leave (released from work, wages still paid to the notice date): employment runs on — enter the day the notice takes effect and answer No. Service counts from the start date through the last day of employment, both counted; an immediate dismissal cannot stretch it to the hypothetical notice date.
Use the last wage rate at dismissal, and only items that are ค่าจ้าง: include a fixed cost-of-living allowance (ค่าครองชีพ); exclude welfare items such as utility or child allowances. Items once received but no longer paid at dismissal do not count. Leave blank if the employee is paid purely by output.
If part of the wage is paid by output (commission, flight hours), enter the amount actually earned in the tier period before the termination effective date. Months with no output still count in the period.
2

The reason for leaving

s.118 pays on employer termination; s.119 causes disqualify.
Result
Severance payable
—
Severance uses the employee's last wage rate per day: monthly ÷ 30, weekly ÷ 7, a daily wage as-is, or a per-period wage ÷ its days. Fixed-term contracts for specific, non-ordinary, time-limited work may be exempt under s.118 ¶3–4 — verify the conditions.
Interest & เงินเพิ่ม (LPA s.9)

Unpaid severance bears 15%/yr during default (s.9 ¶1). Severance is due on dismissal itself, so default runs from the termination effective date (ผิดนัดนับแต่วันเลิกจ้าง). All fields editable.
