Rounding Lab — Simulator & Visualizer

การปัดเศษทุกรูปแบบ — compare all four directed modes (down, up, toward zero, away from zero) and all six round-to-nearest tie-breaking rules on any number, at any decimal precision or increment (nearest 0.25, per 1,000 …). Computed exactly on decimal digits, so 1.005 rounds the way arithmetic says — not the way binary floats drift.

1 · Number & precision
e.g. 1,000 + round up reproduces the stamp-duty rule “ทุกจำนวน 1,000 บาท หรือเศษของ 1,000” — any fraction of a thousand counts as a full thousand.
Enter a number to see every rounding mode side by side.

3 · The staircase — y = round(x)
Filled dot = boundary belongs to that step · open dot = it doesn’t. Lines are nudged apart so overlaps stay visible. The dashed guide marks your number (÷ increment) with its rounded result per mode.
4 · Tie-breaking at a glance
Only exact halves (….5) expose the tie rules — every other value rounds identically under all six “nearest” modes. Shaded cells mark where a rule pulls the tie in its own direction.
Directed modes ignore “nearest” entirely — they always push in their direction, however small the fraction.
5 · Which mode is used where?
Terminology follows the Wikipedia “Rounding” taxonomy. All arithmetic is exact decimal (BigInt) — no binary floating-point surprises.
Companion to the Default Interest and Withholding Tax calculators, which offer normal / floor / ceiling rounding. Runs entirely in your browser.
